01 / What it is

AI wrote the note.
Is every claim in it true?

ClinoTrust splits an AI-written SOAP note into single claims and checks each one against the consultation transcript. Every verdict points to the exact turn and quote, so a clinician reviews only what needs a second look.

02 / Why it matters

Fluent notes can still be wrong.

Softened no

"I don't think so" becomes "denies"

Hedges get flattened into facts.

Wrong section

Patient words filed as exam findings

"No fever observed", when nobody examined anything.

Overstated

"Advise" becomes "prescribe"

Small verb changes carry clinical weight.

04 / How it works

Four steps, no guessing.

1

Generate

An AI scribe writes the SOAP note from the transcript.

2

Split

The note is broken into atomic claims.

3

Verify

A different model checks each claim and must quote the transcript.

4

Review

Quotes are checked verbatim. Anything doubtful is flagged.
